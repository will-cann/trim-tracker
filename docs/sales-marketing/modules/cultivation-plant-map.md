# Cultivation & Plant Map

**Elevator pitch:** See every room and strain group on one map — move plants, flip phases, and flag issues without leaving the floor.

## Who it's for

Cultivation technicians, cultivation managers, directors. Compliance when assigning plant/batch tags.

**Nav:** Plants · Department: cultivation

## Problem

Operators lose track of what's where, what's due to flip or harvest, and which rooms are struggling. Whiteboards and spreadsheets don't scale across rooms and phases.

## Value

One live facility view tied to the same AI that runs harvest and trim. Strain-group health with a real contaminant catalog — not a decorative green ring.

## Key capabilities (shipped)

- Room grid by phase: nursery (batches), vegetative, flowering (individual plants)
- Room cards: plant counts, strain mix, health border color, contaminant abbreviations, flowering week / flip dates
- Bulk actions on strain groups: move, change phase, health report, destroy, schedule harvest
- Create plantings: nursery batches (clone / seed / tissue culture) or labeled plants
- Health 0–100 plus contaminant catalog (mites, PM, botrytis, HLV, etc.) with fixed impact scores
- Health tracked per strain × room group
- Map, data table, and cultivation schedule/calendar views
- Rooms configured in Settings (type, capacity, sq ft, equipment)
- Harvest scheduling from flowering selection; harvest date from strain flowering days on flip

## Status / phases

Plant growth: vegetative → flowering → harvested | destroyed  
Nursery lives as plant batches until up-potted into tracked plants.

## Demo script

1. Open Plant Map → Flowering → expand a room → select a strain → move to another flower room.
2. Flip veg → flower; show harvest date auto-fill from strain flowering days.
3. Flag spider mites + powdery mildew; show health score drop.
4. Voice (AI Home): "Move the OG Kush from veg 2 to flower 1."
5. Schedule harvest with Flower / Fresh Frozen / Both allocation.

## Talking points

- "This isn't a pretty map — it's the same system that weighs plants on harvest day."
- "Health is operator-realistic: strain in a room, not fake per-leaf sensors."
- "Say the move out loud; confirm once; the room counts update."

## Differentiators

- Voice + fuzzy strain/room resolution in one ops app
- Contaminant-driven health scoring (not a manual number only)
- Direct handoff into harvest planning from flowering selection

## Do not claim

- Automated pathogen detection, cameras, or environmental sensors
- Dry/cure as Plant Map phases (dry is a room type; cure lives under Harvest bins)
- Multi-facility plant map
- Live METRC plant sync (tags assignable; API sync is roadmap)
