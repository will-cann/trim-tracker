1. Who we are and what this covers

NeuroCann (“NeuroCann,” “we,” “us”) provides a facility operations platform for licensed cannabis cultivators and processors, available at neurocann.app, through our website, and through integrations with third-party AI assistants (together, the “Service”).

This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website, use the Service, or interact with NeuroCann through an AI assistant. It applies to:

  • Customers — the companies that subscribe to the Service.
  • Users — people who use the Service on behalf of a Customer (owners, managers, technicians, trimmers).
  • Visitors — people who browse our website or use our free planning tools inside an AI assistant without an account.
  • Contacts — people whose details a Customer stores in the Service, such as suppliers, vendors, and store buyers.

NeuroCann acts as a service provider / processor for the operational data a Customer enters into the Service (“Customer Data”). The Customer controls that data and decides how it is used. If you are a User or Contact and have questions about how a Customer uses your information, please contact that Customer directly. For information we collect for our own purposes — such as account registration, website analytics, and billing — NeuroCann is the controller.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information. Name, email address, and a password or single-sign-on identifier, collected through our authentication provider. Your role and department within your company.
  • Company information. Company name, facility and room names, state license numbers, and billing details if you purchase a paid plan.
  • Customer Data. Operational records your team enters or generates: plants, plant batches, strains, harvests and weights, trim sessions and per-trimmer output, extraction runs and yields, packages and inventory adjustments, tags, tasks and assignments, supply levels, purchase orders, and notes. Some of this is personal information about Users (for example, which trimmer processed which batch, who completed a task).
  • Contact records. Names, email addresses, phone numbers, and correspondence with suppliers, vendors, and customers that your team stores in the Service, including email threads sent and received through the Service.
  • Voice and chat input. When you use voice features, audio is streamed to our speech-to-text provider and converted into text. The transcript and your typed requests are processed to carry out what you asked; your conversation history is stored in your browser, not on our servers. We do not retain the audio itself after transcription.
  • Communications with us. Support requests, demo requests, and feedback.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage and device data. Pages and features used, actions taken, timestamps, browser type, operating system, screen size, and approximate location derived from IP address.
  • Diagnostics. Error reports and performance measurements so we can keep the Service reliable. These may include the page you were on and a technical description of the problem.
  • Log data. IP address, request path, and response codes in server logs.

Information from third parties

  • Authentication providers. If you sign in with an identity provider, we receive your name, email, and a unique identifier.
  • AI assistant platforms. When you connect NeuroCann inside ChatGPT, Claude, or another assistant, we receive the authorization you grant and the requests the assistant makes on your behalf. We do not receive your conversation with the assistant beyond the specific tool requests it sends to us.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide, operate, and secure the Service, including authenticating Users and keeping each Customer's data isolated from every other Customer's.
  • Carry out the actions you ask for — recording a harvest weight, creating a task, drafting a supplier email, generating a report.
  • Power AI features: interpret your requests, propose actions for your confirmation, extract tasks from conversation, and answer questions about your facility.
  • Send transactional messages such as team invitations, task reminders, and supplier correspondence you initiate.
  • Provide support, respond to requests, and communicate about changes to the Service.
  • Monitor, debug, and improve performance, reliability, and usability, including through aggregated or de-identified analytics.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We do not use Customer Data to train machine-learning models, whether our own or anyone else's.

4. AI, voice, and assistant integrations

AI is central to how NeuroCann works, so we want to be specific about it.

The in-app assistant and voice features

When you type or speak to the NeuroCann assistant, your request and relevant context from your facility (for example, your room names, active harvests, or strain list) are sent to our AI provider to interpret the request and propose actions. Audio from voice features is sent to our speech-to-text provider for transcription. These providers process the data only to return results to us; under our agreements with them they may not use it to train their models, and they retain it only as needed to provide the service and for abuse monitoring under their published policies.

AI output can be wrong. The Service is designed so that any action an AI proposes is shown to you for review and confirmed by you before it is saved. You remain responsible for verifying information before relying on it, including for regulatory reporting.

Connecting NeuroCann to ChatGPT, Claude, and other assistants

NeuroCann offers a connector (an “MCP server”) so you can use NeuroCann from inside third-party AI assistants.

  • Without signing in, assistants can use our free planning tools (for example, extraction input planning and harvest yield estimates). These tools do not access any account and we do not receive or store personal information about you from them beyond standard server logs.
  • When you sign in and link your NeuroCann account, the assistant can request your facility data and, if you grant write permission, create or complete tasks on your behalf. Each request is authorized with a token you approve through our authentication provider, is limited to the permissions you granted, and is logged.
  • Data you retrieve this way is shared with the assistant's provider (for example, OpenAI for ChatGPT or Anthropic for Claude) and is governed by that provider's privacy policy and your settings with them, including any training or retention choices they offer. NeuroCann does not control how those providers handle data once it is in your conversation.
  • You can disconnect at any time from the assistant's settings or by revoking access in NeuroCann. Revocation takes effect immediately for new requests.

API keys you create for developer tools are stored only as one-way hashes. Anyone with a key can access your company's data within the key's permissions, so treat keys like passwords and revoke any that may have been exposed.

5. How we share information

We share information only as described here:

  • Within your company. Customer Data is visible to Users at your company according to the roles and departments your administrators set.
  • Service providers that process data on our behalf under contracts that restrict their use of it (see Section 6).
  • AI assistant providers you choose to connect, as described in Section 4.
  • Recipients you direct. When you send a supplier email, share a report, or export a transfer file, the recipient receives what you chose to send.
  • Legal and safety. When required by law, subpoena, or regulatory request; to protect the rights, property, or safety of NeuroCann, our Customers, or the public; or to detect and prevent fraud or security issues. Where legally permitted, we will notify the affected Customer of requests for its data.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to this policy's commitments.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

We may share aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably be used to identify you or your company — for example, industry-wide yield benchmarks — and we commit not to re-identify it.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Customer Data is retained for as long as the Customer's account is active. Cannabis operators are typically required to keep production and inventory records for several years; the Customer decides what to retain and may delete records at any time within the Service.
  • After termination, Customer Data is available for export for 30 days, then deleted from our production systems within 60 days, except where we must retain it to comply with law, resolve disputes, or enforce agreements. Backups are overwritten on a rolling schedule within 90 days.
  • Account information is retained while you have an account and deleted on request, except for records we need for security, legal, or accounting purposes.
  • Conversation history with the in-app assistant is stored only in your browser; you can clear it at any time from the Service or by clearing site data. Our servers keep only the records created by actions you confirm (for example, a task or a harvest weight), not the conversation itself.
  • Logs and diagnostics are retained for up to 90 days.

8. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information, including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest for databases and backups.
  • Strict multi-tenant isolation — every database query is scoped to the requesting company.
  • Role- and department-based access controls configured by your administrators.
  • Hashed storage of API keys; short-lived, scoped tokens for assistant integrations.
  • Confirmation steps before AI-proposed actions are saved, and audit attribution on records.
  • Least-privilege access for our staff, who access Customer Data only to support you or operate the Service.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected Customers and regulators as required by applicable law.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. We honor these rights for everyone, regardless of location, to the extent we reasonably can.

  • Users: you can view and update your profile in the Service. For deletion or export, contact your company administrator or us.

  • Contacts: if a Customer stores your details in the Service, direct requests to that Customer; we will assist them in responding.
  • Marketing: we send marketing email only with your consent, and every message includes an unsubscribe link. Transactional messages (invites, task reminders) are part of the Service.

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we have not done so in the preceding 12 months. You may exercise your rights to know, delete, correct, and limit use of sensitive personal information by contacting us; we will not discriminate against you for doing so. You may use an authorized agent with proof of authorization.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland: our legal bases are performance of a contract (providing the Service), legitimate interests (security, analytics, improvement), consent (marketing, optional cookies), and legal obligation. You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

To exercise any right, email will@neurocann.app. We will verify your identity, respond within 30 days (or as applicable law requires), and tell you if we need more time.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and browser storage that are strictly necessary to operate the Service — keeping you signed in, remembering preferences, protecting against cross-site request forgery — and local storage on your device for conversation history and offline resilience. Our error-monitoring provider sets a technical identifier to group reports from the same session.

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels. If we add analytics cookies that are not strictly necessary, we will ask for consent where required and update this section. You can clear cookies and site data through your browser; doing so will sign you out.

11. International transfers

NeuroCann is operated from the United States and our providers store data in the United States. If you access the Service from elsewhere, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States. Where required, we rely on contractual safeguards such as standard contractual clauses with our providers.

12. Age requirements

The Service is a business tool for licensed cannabis operations and is not directed to anyone under the legal age to work in that industry in their jurisdiction, and never to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.
